# Understanding B.R.E.A.D.: How We Score Every Card

If you've read one of our [pre-release articles](/prerelease), you've seen the B.R.E.A.D. section — every card in the set sorted into five buckets before we talk about anything else. This post explains what B.R.E.A.D. actually is, where it comes from, and why we use it as the backbone of our card scoring.

## What B.R.E.A.D. Is

B.R.E.A.D. is a limited-format pick-order heuristic that's been part of drafting culture for years. The idea is simple: when you're staring at a pack and don't know what any of these cards are worth yet, sort them by what they *do* rather than what they cost. The acronym gives you a priority order:

1. **Bombs** — cards that win the game by themselves
2. **Removal** — answers that take a threat off the board
3. **Evasion** — ways to get damage through blockers
4. **Aggro** — pressure on the opponent's life total
5. **Duds** — the rest

It's not a scientific ranking so much as a gut-check: when two cards are close, the one higher on this list is usually the better pick.

## Our Version

We use the same five letters, but the last category is different. Instead of "Duds" — cards with no clear job — we score for **Diversity**: card advantage, life gain, and ramp. Every good deck needs a handful of these "glue" cards even though they never make a highlight reel, so we'd rather measure what a card *contributes* than just what it lacks. Here's how we define each bucket:

- **Bomb** — Big creatures that can close out the game on their own. Ward, Indestructible, Hexproof, and Trample make a bomb even harder to answer.
- **Removal** — Answers that take an opponent's threat off the board. Ranked Exile > Destroy > temporary exile, since the more permanent the answer, the less it can be undone.
- **Evasion** — Ways to get damage through despite blockers — unblockable, flying, trample, and menace, roughly in that order of strength.
- **Aggro** — Anything that pressures life total directly: pingers, buffs, haste, trample, and double strike.
- **Diversity** — Card advantage, life gain, and ramp — the reliable "filler" cards every good deck still needs.

A card can land in more than one bucket if it genuinely does more than one job — a flying bomb with removal attached scores in all three.

## How We Use It

In every pre-release article, we run the entire set through this same rules-based scorer before we've played a single game with the cards. It's not a substitute for actual play experience, but it's a consistent, repeatable lens for comparing hundreds of unfamiliar cards at once — which colors are stacked with removal, which archetypes are light on bombs, where the aggro payoffs cluster. From there we layer on mana curve, synergy, and archetype analysis to build the full picture.

Next time you're looking at a B.R.E.A.D. breakdown in one of our set articles, you'll know exactly what you're looking at — and why we chose Diversity over Duds.
