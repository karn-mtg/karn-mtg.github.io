## Before you crack a pack

Lorwyn Eclipsed brings tight, even color balance across the 232 cards in our pull — no color is
starved, and multicolor cards are the biggest single bucket. Colorless is small at 10 cards.

## Where the power actually lives

Champions of the Perfect tops the board as the set's standout card. White-Red is the strongest
two-color pair by raw power here, anchored by Hovel Hurler and Bre of Clan Stoutarm. Graveyard/Mill
Matters is the far more reliable build-around signal if you want a plan likely to actually come
together in a sealed pool. Use the explorer below to dig into individual cards by tag, color, or
rarity.
