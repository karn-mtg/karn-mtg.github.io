## Before you crack a pack

The Hobbits is a smaller, tighter set than Final Fantasy — 193 cards against 312 — and the color
balance shows it: no color is starved, and no color dominates either. That evenness is worth
knowing going into a draft or sealed pool, since it means color openness will likely come down to
what's actually in your packs, not a structural bias in the set.

## Where the power actually lives

Beorn the Fierce tops the board as a pure bomb — no secondary tag, just a big, hard-to-answer
threat. Right behind it, Old Fat Spider and Smaug, Wicked Worm both combine bomb-level stats with
evasion, and Pinecone Strike is the set's standout pure removal spell. Head of the Hunt is the
"bomb that's also removal" pattern again: a big body that answers a card on the way in.

**Black/Green** is comfortably the strongest two-color pair by average bomb strength here, well
ahead of the next pair (Black/Red), anchored by Chief Warg's Company and Large Bear. If you're
looking for the highest floor on raw stats per card, that's the pair to prioritize — everything
else in this set trails it by a wide margin. Use the explorer below to dig into individual cards
by tag, color, or rarity.
