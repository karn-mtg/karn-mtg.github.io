## Before you crack a pack

Marvel Super Heroes packs 267 cards into our current pull, with multicolor cards as the clear
backbone of the set — this is a "commit to two colors" set more than a "stay mono and splash"
set. Colorless sits small at just 20 cards.

## Where the power actually lives

Thor, God of Thunder tops the board as the set's standout card. Black-Green is comfortably the
strongest two-color pair by raw power, anchored by Titania, Rugged Rumbler and The Serpent
Society — if you're looking for the highest floor on raw stats per card, that's the pair to
prioritize. Use the explorer below to dig into individual cards by tag, color, or rarity.
