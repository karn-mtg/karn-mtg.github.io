## Before you crack a pack

This article exists to answer one question before prerelease weekend: what is this set actually
made of? Not the flavor-text version — the numbers. Everything below is computed straight from
the card database for Final Fantasy, then scored with a simple, transparent heuristic (fully
documented in the site's `bread-rules.json`, tunable if you disagree with a call).

## What the color split tells us

Every color sits close to 42–47 cards, so this isn't a set where one color is starved — the real
story is the **multicolor slot**. At 57 cards and 46 of them creatures, Final Fantasy is clearly
building toward two-color decks as the default, not the exception. Colorless (31 cards, mostly
non-creature — artifacts and Vehicles) rounds out the support shell.

## Where the power actually lives

Ragnarok, Divine Deliverance tops the overall board, and it's a good example of why B.R.E.A.D.
scores multiple categories at once: it's tagged as a bomb, a removal spell, an evasive threat,
*and* an aggro card, because that's genuinely what the card does. Coliseum Behemoth and Ancient
Adamantoise aren't far behind — big bodies that also answer a card on the way in, the exact
"bomb that's also removal" pattern the B.R.E.A.D. framework is built to surface.

Looking at two-color pairs by average bomb strength, **White/Blue** and **Red/Green** currently
lead the set (Absolute Virtue and Cid, Timeless Artificer anchoring WU; Gladiolus Amicitia and
Balthier and Fran anchoring RG), with **Black/Green** close behind on the back of Ragnarok itself.
