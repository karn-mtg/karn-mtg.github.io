## Before you crack a pack

Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles is a tighter set than most in our coverage — 186 cards — with
multicolor cards clearly the backbone of the set's design. Colorless sits small at 12 cards.

## Where the power actually lives

Krang, Utrom Warlord tops the board as the set's standout card. The raw color-pair ranking here
comes with a big caveat: the top pair by average bomb (Red-Green) is propped up by a single card
with a low pull chance, so don't over-index on it. Graveyard/Mill Matters is the far more reliable
signal — build around it if you want a plan that's actually likely to come together. Use the
