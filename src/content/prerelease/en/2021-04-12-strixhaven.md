## Before you crack a pack

Strixhaven leans multicolor more than most sets in our coverage — the college identities are
built into the card pool itself, not just the archetypes on top of it. Mono colors trail well
behind gold cards here.

## Where the power actually lives

Bookwurm tops the board as the set's standout card. Black-Green is comfortably the strongest
two-color pair by raw power, and — unusually for our coverage — it's backed by real depth rather
than one or two standout cards: 12 supporting cards with a near-certain pull chance. Use the
